# Review and MFA authorization laboratory
No public endpoint. Staff role must be enabled. For community review, require an actual fresh MFA verification (TOTP or WebAuthn UV) bound to authenticated actor and server-verified session, purpose, <=5-minute lifetime, single use. The database owner inserts synthetic proofs in tests only. Do not let clients or general app role create proofs. Actual MFA verifier and proof issuer, session revocation checks, restricted reviewer role, all other administrator operations, owner recovery, appeals, moderation queue, and append-only audit permissions remain prerequisites. This is not complete site-wide admin MFA. Hidden community posts are retained, not deleted. Real role mapping and behavior require owner approval. No production changes or paid calls.

## Identity case response boundary
The isolated metadata queue validates server-returned case IDs, filter scope, cursor exclusion, allowed states, evidence kinds and timestamps. It suppresses expired evidence metadata and rechecks the same verified session after committing the single-use proof and audit, before returning the page. Revoked or changed sessions receive no case data. This is a secondary fail-closed check, not instant revocation propagation; actual reviewer role revocation and provider integration still require live acceptance. No raw identity materials, public route or new service enabled.
