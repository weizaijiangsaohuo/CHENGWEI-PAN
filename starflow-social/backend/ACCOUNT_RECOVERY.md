# Fresh-account recovery — phases 2 and 4
User-requested entry routes: account identifier → safe account selection → live face verification against that account's enrolled reference → new password; verified identity information → safe account selection → same ceremony. Neither route can restore a retired legacy account or any old privilege.
Before showing even masked candidates, require independent proof of an enrolled contact or recovery code; unknown, ambiguous and unauthorized requests return indistinguishable responses. Identity information is not a secret or authenticator. No public name/ID search or bulk account discovery. Use opaque random selection IDs and bounded attempts, issuer-side rate limits, no identifiers in URLs or logs. Do not log or retain raw captures by default; obtain separate informed consent, define revocation and deletion, and provide a non-biometric supervised alternative for users who cannot or do not consent to facial recovery. No production biometric collection in this change.
For the selected active fresh account only, bind an authenticated provider session to exact attempt, auth subject and original independently verified enrollment reference. Active liveness plus one-to-one match against that enrolled reference is required, not a new document matched to a new selfie. Existing Didit enrollment evaluator matches the document from its own session and cannot serve as this recovery proof. Verify actual vendor capabilities, signed/authenticated results and zero-cost eligibility; no paid calls or downgraded liveness. No new provider fields are assumed by the normalized offline predicate.
The predicate in recovery-ceremony.mjs is only a sandbox evidence check; it does not issue an authorization or persist state. Actual redemption needs short-lived opaque one-use proof, transactional CAS consumption and current binding/consent/staff/MFA eligibility recheck with coordinated token minting, password update and all-session revocation. Disable direct email-only reset routes under the new policy. Keep MFA/staff recovery on a separate stronger, audited path; face cannot remove their additional authentication factors or restore management rights. No reset route activation before provider integration, database atomicity, privacy review and end-to-end tests.

## Fresh provenance preflight (027)
Ordinary recovery preflight now requires an already-consumed fresh registration admission in addition to the existing explicit policy, verified email, exact subject and absence of MFA/staff. A historical binding alone or an unfinished signup is insufficient. Consumed admission is a necessary provenance marker only, not proof of identity, possession, consent, facial enrollment or authorization to reset a password. Test fixtures verify legacy/unconsumed denial, consent-policy withdrawal, email mismatch, MFA/staff exclusion, and restricted gateway access. No route, biometric capture or password mutation is added. Apply on staging only after acceptance and leave public recovery disabled.
